# 60-second demo video script

Use synthetic or non-sensitive demo evidence only.

## 0-8 seconds — problem

Show the BGNexa README or landing screen.

Narration:

> "Evidence-heavy GRC work often mixes scope, evidence, judgement, and follow-up into one opaque workflow. BGNexa separates those steps."

## 8-25 seconds — evidence to readiness

Open the live beta and show a non-sensitive demo assessment.

Narration:

> "BGNexa ingests evidence, keeps source provenance visible, retrieves relevant material, and separates provisional readiness from resolved coverage."

## 25-42 seconds — assurance workspace

Show the Assurance Workspace.

Narration:

> "The assurance workspace turns applicable controls into traceable work: evidence requests, test procedures, owners, findings, actions, retest, and closure."

## 42-52 seconds — trust boundaries

Show evidence quality/freshness or provider settings.

Narration:

> "Applicability stays deterministic, evidence quality and freshness remain visible, and optional external AI never becomes the authority for compliance."

## 52-60 seconds — call to action

Show GitHub.

Narration:

> "Try the public beta with non-sensitive data, star BGNexa if the workflow is useful, fork it to test new assurance ideas, or contribute a focused pull request."

## Recording notes

- Do not show real client/regulator-confidential evidence.
- Do not say BGNexa certifies compliance or issues audit opinions.
- Keep the video focused on one workflow, not the full feature list.
