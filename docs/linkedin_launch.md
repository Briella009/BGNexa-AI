# LinkedIn launch draft — BGNexa AI public beta

I’ve been building **BGNexa AI**, an open-source evidence intelligence and assurance workspace for cybersecurity, privacy and regulatory readiness.

The original question was simple: why do GRC and internal-audit teams still spend so much time manually sorting policies, procedures, logs, reports and control evidence before they can even begin a meaningful review?

BGNexa AI is my attempt to make that workflow more structured without pretending AI can replace professional judgement.

The current public beta can:

- map uploaded evidence to selected cybersecurity/privacy requirements;
- distinguish documented intent from operational, technical, audit/test, contractual, training and regulatory-filing evidence;
- flag stale, weak, missing and potentially unsafe evidence;
- preserve human validation instead of treating AI output as a compliance conclusion;
- surface priority evidence gaps and remediation work;
- identify potential evidence reuse across frameworks without claiming control equivalence;
- turn readiness results into an **Assurance Workspace** for evidence requests, control testing, findings, management actions, retest and closure;
- export traceable readiness and assurance records.

Current framework coverage includes selected readiness content for the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, NDPC GAID 2025, CBN cybersecurity frameworks for OFIs and DMBs/PSBs, ISO/IEC 27001 clause-level readiness, NIST CSF 2.0 and GDPR.

One design decision matters to me: **BGNexa does not call a company “compliant.”** It reports what the supplied evidence supports, what remains unresolved, and what a qualified reviewer should look at next.

The project now has automated regression tests, source-provenance checks, deterministic retrieval evaluations and prompt-injection safeguards. The public beta is still a research build, so I’m specifically looking for feedback from GRC analysts, internal auditors, cybersecurity assurance professionals, privacy teams and control owners.

If you work in any of those areas, I’d be interested in one question in particular:

**What part of evidence collection, control testing or remediation follow-up wastes the most time in your current workflow?**

## Shorter alternative

I’ve just moved **BGNexa AI** into public beta.

It started as an evidence-backed cybersecurity/privacy readiness tool. It has now grown into an assurance workflow for GRC and internal-audit teams: evidence intake, control mapping, human validation, evidence requests, testing, findings, management actions, retest and closure.

The important part is what it **doesn’t** do: it doesn’t declare an organisation compliant, issue an audit opinion or let an LLM decide regulatory applicability. It shows what the evidence supports and what still needs qualified review.

I’m opening it up for feedback from GRC, internal audit, cybersecurity assurance, privacy and control-owner teams.
